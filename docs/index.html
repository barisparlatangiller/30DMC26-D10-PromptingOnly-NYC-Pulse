About this map

Day. Tuesday 16 June 2026, 00:00–23:59 New York time (EDT). TLC and MTA timestamps are local time.

Trips. NYC TLC trip records for Yellow taxi, Green taxi and high-volume for-hire vehicles (Uber, Lyft). A pickup counts in the 10 minutes it starts, a drop-off in the 10 minutes it ends. Removed: unknown zones (264/265), trips under 1 minute or over 4 hours.

Subway. MTA Subway Hourly Ridership: entries per station complex and hour, all fare types.

Heart. The trip-weighted centre of the taxi zones in that hour (pickups + drop-offs), and the entry-weighted centre of the subway stations. It is a weighted centre of these trips, not where people are and not the single centre of New York. Airport trips pull the taxi heart east.

Limits. Taxi and ride-hail trips are not all traffic and not all people. Arcs connect zone centres; they are not real routes. The animation replays measured data; it is not a forecast.
