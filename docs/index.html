About this map

Day. Tuesday 16 June 2026, 00:00–23:59 New York time (EDT). TLC and MTA timestamps are local time.

Trips. NYC TLC trip records for Yellow taxis, Green "Boro" taxis (street hails only outside central Manhattan) and high-volume for-hire vehicles (Uber, Lyft). The toggle "Taxis" covers yellow and green. A pickup counts in the 10 minutes it starts, a drop-off in the 10 minutes it ends. Removed: unknown zones (264/265), trips under 1 minute or over 4 hours.

Subway. MTA Subway Hourly Ridership: entries per station complex and hour, all fare types.

Hearts. A heart marks where trips are densest at that moment: a kernel density of pickups + drop-offs at the taxi-zone centres (1 km radius), and its peak. The city heart uses all car trips; the borough hearts only the zones of Brooklyn, Queens and the Bronx; the subway heart uses station entries. A plain average position was tried first: because most trips start outside Manhattan, it fell into the East River, so the density peak is used instead. Hearts show where trips concentrate, not where people are.

Limits. Taxi and ride-hail trips are not all traffic and not all people. TLC records only start and end zones, so each moving light follows the fastest route on today's OpenStreetMap road network between the two zone centres, using road-class speeds: a model, not the real route. The animation replays measured data; it is not a forecast.
